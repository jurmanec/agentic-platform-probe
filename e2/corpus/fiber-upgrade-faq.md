# Fiber upgrade FAQ

Illustrative product FAQ for a residential fiber upgrade campaign.

## What is the fiber upgrade?

Existing residential customers may be eligible for a higher-speed fiber plan in supported Northeast markets. The campaign promotes awareness and self-serve upgrade paths.

## Who is eligible?

Active residential accounts in the Northeast region that are not already on the top fiber tier. Business accounts are out of scope for this FAQ.

## How do customers upgrade?

1. Open the account portal or click the campaign email CTA.
2. Review available fiber tiers for their address.
3. Confirm the upgrade; changes typically activate within 1–2 business days.

## Messaging guidance

- Emphasize reliability and speed for work-from-home and streaming.
- Do not promise specific dollar discounts unless a priced offer ID is attached to the journey step.
- Do not claim coverage outside the Northeast residential footprint described in the campaign brief.
